# AutoLux 100-vehicle requirement — Phase 3 delivery status

The Phase 3 catalogue now contains 100 distinct manufacturer/model records and the import/migration foundation. These records are demo catalogue data, not verified physical AutoLux inventory.

## Delivered in Phase 3
- 100 distinct vehicle models with categories, year, fuel, transmission, seating and stock identifiers.
- Separate rental guide prices and sale-market reference prices, each with a declared basis and source URL/date.
- Browser-side Wikimedia Commons image discovery that only displays images with reusable license classes and links to source/attribution. Image files are not bundled or pre-verified one-by-one in the ZIP.
- Public catalogue, vehicle detail page and role-protected fleet management.
- Admin overview dashboard and vehicle status audit trail.

## Still required before public commercial launch
1. Confirm the exact year, trim, condition and specification of every physical vehicle AutoLux actually owns or is authorized to list.
2. Review the chosen Wikimedia image for model/year accuracy. Confirm license and attribution on the original file page. If no suitable image is found, upload a properly licensed AutoLux image to company storage.
3. Replace category estimates and public market medians with actual AutoLux sale prices and approved rental prices by branch, date, vehicle and contract policy.
4. Assign each physical unit to a real branch and record registration, VIN, mileage, insurance and inspection details in the later inventory phase.
5. Keep demo records marked as demo until verified; do not treat a catalogue model record as proof of fleet ownership.

## Pricing sources used for planning
- Used vehicle market guide: https://www.carwhere.com/used-car-prices
- Rental fleet examples: https://www.rochester.rentals/fleet
- Premium rental examples: https://rent-trip.com/usa/premium/

Prices vary with location, date, season, trim, mileage, condition, taxes, fees and supplier. They are planning references, not binding AutoLux offers.
